# The Gawain Romances (English, 14th–15th c.) — the code on one knight

The popular English tradition that puts not the whole order but a *single knight* under the
chivalric test — *Sir Gawain and the Green Knight* (the masterpiece) plus the homelier romances
Sir Frederic Madden gathered in 1839 (the Wedding of Dame Ragnelle, the Awntyrs, Golagros, the
Carle of Carlisle).

## The apparatus
- **Sir Gawain and the Green Knight** — the beheading-game and the year-long test of one knight's
  *truth*; the flaw is the green girdle he keeps for fear of death — `sggk-green-girdle` [SGGK]:
  *"For quat gome so is gorde with þis grene lace … þer is no haþel vnder heuen to-hewe hym þat
  my3t."* The one lapse in an almost-perfect knight: chivalry audited down to a single concealment.
- **The Wedding of Sir Gawain and Dame Ragnelle** — the Loathly Lady and the riddle,
  `ragnelle-riddle`: *"Whate wemen desyren moste, in good faye, my lyf els shold I lese,"* whose
  answer is **sovereignty**, `ragnelle-sovereignty`: *"to haue the soveraynté … of alle, bothe hyghe
  and lowe."* Sovereignty moved from the throne to the marriage-bed (Cut 1, inverted and comic).
  Madden's 1839 blackletter is too rough for Tier-1 (it scores 0.000 against the raw scan), so these
  are cited from a provenance-stamped **cleaned derivative** (`cleaned/ragnelle.txt`; the OCR policy
  is in `docs/bases/_engine/README.md`). The other Madden romances (Awntyrs, Golagros, Carle) stay
  searchable-but-rough.
- **The Awntyrs off Arthure** (Guinevere's mother's ghost — a warning of the fall), **Golagros and
  Gawane** (the limits of conquest), **Syre Gawene and the Carle of Carlisle** (the comic
  beheading-test analogue) — all in Madden; searchable, rough OCR.

## Steelman
Where the French anatomize the whole order, the English put the code on trial in *one conscience* —
and Gawain's single failure, honestly confessed, is a subtler verdict on chivalry than any battle.
Ragnelle adds the canon's sharpest joke about power: what everyone secretly wants is to *rule*.

## The standard objection
The minor romances are uneven and survive in poor copies (Ragnelle in a single bad manuscript), and
the 1839 OCR compounds it. Cite SGGK (clean Middle English) freely; treat the Madden romances as
searchable-but-rough until re-sourced or cleaned.

## How it maps

The English popular counterpoint to the French courtly anatomies; kin to Malory, the English spine.
